# Landing image assets

Generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool on 2026-08-21, then
converted to WebP for production delivery. The generated source PNG files remain
in Codex's generated-image workspace; only the optimized WebP assets are used by
the application.

## Prompt set

### `hero-bakery.webp`

Photorealistic editorial photograph of a warm contemporary Korean neighborhood
bakery near closing time. Fresh salt bread and croissants fill the foreground,
with a kraft pickup box and a baker's hands finishing an order. Warm cream,
toasted brown, soft orange, and light oak palette; natural late-afternoon light;
wide landing-hero composition. No visible face, text, price tags, logos,
watermarks, UI, or exaggerated stock-photo styling.

### `salt-bread.webp`

Photorealistic product-card photograph of exactly three glossy Korean salt
breads in a kraft tray on a warm cream stone tabletop. Natural butter sheen,
salted tops, linen, and realistic crumbs; square crop. No text, labels, logos,
hands, price cards, watermarks, or artificial-looking food.

### `croissant.webp`

Photorealistic product-card photograph of two flaky butter croissants and one
small pain au chocolat in an open kraft pickup box on a light oak counter.
Natural laminated texture, warm side light, square crop. No text, labels, logos,
hands, price cards, watermarks, or luxury-hotel styling.

### `dessert-box.webp`

Photorealistic product-card photograph of an open pickup box containing two
fruit tartlets, one slice of Basque cheesecake, and two madeleines. Warm
off-white tabletop, gentle daylight, realistic texture, square crop. No text,
labels, logos, hands, price cards, watermarks, or neon colors.

### `city-map.webp`

Clean top-down cartographic illustration of a dense, walkable Korean-style urban
neighborhood. Warm ivory land, light beige streets, muted sage parks, and pale
blue water; wide 16:10 composition with calm central space for HTML markers and
cards. No labels, numbers, pins, logos, map-provider styling, controls, or
watermarks.
